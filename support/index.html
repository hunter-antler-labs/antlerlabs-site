Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Flush keeps your dog, your level, your trophies and your settings in a file on your iPhone. Nothing is stored online.

What happens if I delete the app?

Your progress goes with it. Deleting Flush erases the save file, and there's no copy on our end to restore it from. If you install the game again, you'll start over with a new pup.

Does Flush collect any of my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The privacy policy has the details.
