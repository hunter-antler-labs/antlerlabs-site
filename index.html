On Point: Upland Bird Hunting

Raise a bird dog from a pup and take it hunting for pheasant, chukar and quail in real places, each with its own birds and its own kind of cover.

Your dog works the field ahead of you. When the birds flush, tap to shoot. The more you hunt together, the more your dog learns.
