On Point: Upland Bird Hunting

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

On Point: Upland Bird Hunting is made by Antler Labs LLC. This page explains what the game does with your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. On Point doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or anything else about you, and there's no account to sign up for. No personal data leaves your device because of On Point.

No ads, analytics or tracking

There are no ads in On Point. The game doesn't use analytics services, tracking tools or any outside code that records what you do. We don't follow you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share data with anyone, because we don't have any to share.

Your game progress

Your dog, your level, the birds you've taken and your settings are saved in a file on your device. That file isn't sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete On Point, the file is deleted along with it.

Apple

You get On Point from the App Store, so Apple handles your purchase and payment under its own privacy policy. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers on your iPhone, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and basic usage numbers. They don't identify you. You can turn that off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

On Point doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If On Point ever starts collecting any data, this page will say so before that update comes out.
